RLG Introduction

Royal London Group (often abbreviated as RLG or Royal London) is the largest mutual life insurance, pensions, and investment company in the United Kingdom.

Core Products

  • Pensions & Retirement: Personal pensions, workplace pensions, and annuities.
  • Life Insurance & Protection: Critical illness cover, income protection, and whole-of-life insurance.
  • Asset Management: Operates Royal London Asset Management (RLAM), managing active investments across global equities, fixed income, and real estate.
  • Subsidiaries & Brands: Historically acquired major UK financial brands including Scottish Life, Scottish Provident, and Bright Grey to expand its life and pensions footprint.
